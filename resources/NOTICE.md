# Ligand–receptor resource

`LR_pairs_cellchat_omnipath.tsv` combines ligand–receptor records from CellChat and OmniPath.
It has 8,116 entries: 1,561 from CellChat only, 4,882 from OmniPath only and 1,673 from both
(column `source`).
